# Anatomical & Kinesiological Guide to Motive Movement Silhouettes

This guide details the kinesiology, joint articulation, and natural human motion dynamics for each of the 13 movement patterns in Motive. It serves as the foundation for translating real human movement into authentic vector silhouettes.

---

## 1. Squat (`squat`)
*Covering: Air Squat, Bulgarian Split Squat, Shrimp Squat, Pistol Squat*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Phase 1: Neutral Stance (Tadasana / Mountain Foundation)**
  - Feet shoulder-width apart, turned out 5–15° naturally.
  - Spine neutral with natural cervical lordosis, thoracic kyphosis, and lumbar lordosis.
  - Center of mass centered over midfoot; arms resting naturally at sides or poised in front.
- **Phase 2: Hinge & Descent (Eccentric)**
  - Initiation begins simultaneously at hips and knees: hips break back (anterior pelvic tilt into hip flexion) while knees track directly over 2nd/3rd toes.
  - Torso tilts forward ~30–40° from vertical as a necessary counterbalance, keeping the combined center of gravity directly over the feet.
  - Arms rise smoothly forward to horizontal, acting as a dynamic counterweight.
- **Phase 3: Deep Inversion / Hole (Peak)**
  - Femurs reach or break parallel to the ground; knees flex to ~110–120°.
  - Chest remains proud and open; lumbar spine stays neutral without posterior pelvic tuck ("butt wink").
- **Phase 4: Drive & Extension (Concentric)**
  - Gluteus maximus, quadriceps, and adductor magnus contract synergistically.
  - Hips and shoulders rise at the same velocity until full triple extension (ankles, knees, hips) locks out at the top.

### Silhouette Translation
- Contoured thighs showing quadriceps swell and hamstring taper.
- Curved gastrocnemius (calf) narrowing to Achilles tendon.
- Counterbalanced arms extending straight forward at parallel depth.

## 2. Pull (`pull`)
*Covering: Pronated, Neutral, Commando, Chin-up, Wide, Scapular Pull-ups*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Phase 1: Full Dead Hang**
  - Arms in full overhead flexion; scapulae upwardly rotated and elevated.
  - Core in mild "hollow body" tension (ribs down, legs angled slightly forward 5–10° to prevent lumbar hyperextension).
- **Phase 2: Scapular Depression & Retraction**
  - Lower trapezius and latissimus dorsi fire first, pulling shoulder blades down before elbow flexion begins.
- **Phase 3: Concentric Drive**
  - Elbows drive aggressively down toward the ribcage and back.
  - Sternum angles upward toward the bar (~15° backward body lean); cervical spine remains neutral without chin reaching/straining.
- **Phase 4: Apex Lockout**
  - Chin clears the bar plane; forearms at ~45–60° angle; lats, teres major, and biceps in peak peak contraction.
- **Phase 5: Controlled Return**
  - Smooth eccentric deceleration returning through active hang into dead hang.

### Silhouette Translation
- Broad inverted-triangle silhouette (V-taper from latissimus dorsi to waist).
- Clear elbow flexion and forearm angles.
- Legs held together with pointed toes in clean gymnastic tension.

## 3. Push & Dip (`push`)
*Covering: Tricep Dips, Chest Dips*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Phase 1: Top Support Lockout**
  - Shoulders depressed away from ears (pectoralis minor, serratus anterior, lower traps).
  - Elbows locked in extension; torso vertical (tricep focus) or inclined 15° forward (chest focus).
- **Phase 2: Controlled Descent**
  - Torso leans slightly forward to balance center of mass; elbows flex backward keeping close to the body.
  - Descent halts when upper arms (humerus) reach parallel to parallel bars (~90° flexion).
- **Phase 3: Concentric Press**
  - Triceps brachii, anterior deltoids, and sternal pectoralis drive the handles downwards, raising the body back to full lockout.

### Silhouette Translation
- Strong upper body angle with bent elbows creating a clean, angular silhouette.
- Lower legs crossed or held in gentle backward flexion.

## 4. Jump (`jump`)
*Covering: Squat Jump*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Phase 1: Countermovement Loading**
  - Rapid drop into a quarter squat; torso pitches forward; arms swing back behind hips into shoulder extension.
- **Phase 2: Triple Extension Propulsion**
  - Explosive extension through ankles (plantarflexion), knees, and hips.
  - Arms swing forward and up, transferring upward momentum into the torso.
- **Phase 3: Airborne Flight Phase**
  - Total vertical elongation; feet leave ground with toes pointed downward; floating suspension.
- **Phase 4: Soft Deceleration Landing**
  - Forefeet strike first, then heels contact; ankles, knees, and hips flex smoothly to dissipate kinetic energy.

### Silhouette Translation
- Dynamic vertical posture with toes pointed down and arms elevated, creating the visual impression of weightlessness.

## 5. Core (`core`)
*Covering: Hanging Knee Raise, Toes-to-Bar*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Phase 1: Active Overhead Hang**
  - Scapulae engaged to stabilize the glenohumeral joint; torso long.
- **Phase 2: Pelvic Rotation & Flexion**
  - Rectus abdominis and iliopsoas contract; pelvis tucks posteriorly (rounding lower back forward) before thighs elevate.
- **Phase 3: Peak Compression**
  - Thighs compress against ribcage (knee raise) or shins/toes reach the bar (toes-to-bar).
  - Head stays positioned between shoulders; no neck hyperextension.
- **Phase 4: Eccentric Unfurling**
  - Controlled lowering without swinging pendulum momentum.

### Silhouette Translation
- Curled ball or L-shape profile demonstrating strong abdominal compression against vertical hang line.

## 6. Bridge (`bridge`)
*Covering: Glute Bridge, Hand Bridge, Head Bridge (Setu Bandhasana / Urdhva Dhanurasana)*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Phase 1: Supine Grounding**
  - Back on mat, knees flexed ~90°, heels beneath knees, shoulder blades anchored.
- **Phase 2: Pelvic Lift & Hip Extension**
  - Gluteus maximus and hamstrings contract; pelvis tilts posteriorly; spine articulates off the floor vertebra by vertebra.
- **Phase 3: Full Thoracic Wheel / Arch**
  - In hand bridge (Wheel): hands plant beside ears; shoulders flex overhead and elbows extend, driving heart and thoracic cage open.
  - Continuous parabolic arc across thoracic and lumbar vertebrae, avoiding isolated pinching in L5-S1.

### Silhouette Translation
- Fluid, harmonious arc silhouette; grounded hands and feet framing the curved arch of the spine.

---

## 7. Stretch (`stretch`)
*Covering: Wrist Extensor Stretch, Wrist Flexor Stretch, Joint Prep*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Posture & Alignment**: Seated kneeling (Virasana / Hero Pose) or quadruped position.
- **Action**: Torso held tall with effortless spinal alignment; shoulders relaxed; arms extended with focused palm/wrist articulation; deep, measured diaphragmatic breathing.

### Silhouette Translation
- Contemplative seated profile showing tall spine, open chest, and deliberate wrist flexion/extension.

---

## 8. Roll (`roll`)
*Covering: Rolling Ball (Pawanmuktasana)*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Posture**: Compact anatomical sphere.
- **Action**: Forearms clasp shins, chin tucks toward sternum, spine curves into an unbroken C-shape.
- **Dynamic**: Smooth kinetic roll backward along the spinal erectors from sacrum to upper thoracic spine, returning on the rebound to balanced seated equilibrium without feet touching the ground.

### Silhouette Translation
- Smooth circular droplet/egg silhouette, conveying momentum, softness, and spinal protection.

---

## 9. Fold (`fold`)
*Covering: Plow Stretch (Halasana)*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Posture**: Inversion forward fold.
- **Action**: Supine body elevates hips vertically over shoulders; straight legs travel overhead until toes touch floor behind crown.
- **Safety**: Weight rests across shoulder girdle (acromion processes and upper back), keeping the cervical vertebrae completely uncompressed and free. Hands support lower back or clasp mat.

### Silhouette Translation
- Elegant hairpin silhouette with inverted torso and long straight legs extending parallel to or touching the floor.

---

## 10. Balance (`balance`)
*Covering: Crow Hold (Bakasana), Tripod Headstand, Standing Back Raise, Wall Walk*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Crow Pose (Bakasana)**:
  - Palms flat shoulder-width; fingers spread wide gripping the floor (hasta bandha).
  - Elbows slightly bent to form a shelf; knees nestle into triceps/armpits.
  - Center of mass tilts forward over wrists; feet lighten and float up toward glutes; head lifts slightly with serene gaze (drishti) forward.
- **Tree Pose / Standing Balance**:
  - Single standing foot roots into ground; opposite foot presses into inner thigh; arms join overhead in Anjali mudra.

### Silhouette Translation
- Compact perched silhouette in midair equilibrium, conveying weightlessness, poise, and stillness.

---

## 11. Hold (`hold`)
*Covering: Reverse Plank (Purvottanasana)*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Posture**: Supine extended plank.
- **Action**: Hands plant beneath shoulders pointing toward heels; heels dig into ground; glutes and hamstrings contract vigorously to elevate pelvis.
- **Alignment**: Unbroken diagonal line from heels to shoulders; chest expanded; scapulae retracted and depressed; neck in line with spine.

### Silhouette Translation
- Pure diagonal silhouette elevated cleanly off the ground line, conveying isometric strength and spaciousness.

---

## 12. Run (`run`)
*Covering: Slow Run Zone-1, Fast Uphill Run, Interval Run, High Knees*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Gait Dynamics**:
  - Slight forward lean pivoting from the ankles (not the waist).
  - Reciprocal arm swing: elbows bent at ~90°, swinging forward and back in rhythm with opposing leg.
  - Drive leg: knee drives forward and upward; ankle dorsiflexes.
  - Support leg: drives straight down and back through hip extension; elastic push-off via Achilles tendon.
  - Airborne flight phase where neither foot contacts the ground.

### Silhouette Translation
- Dynamic athletic posture with leading knee high, trailing leg extended in push-off, and expressive arm drive.

---

## 13. Walk (`walk`)
*Covering: Power Walk Downhill, Slow Walk Zone-1 (Kinhin)*

### Natural Kinesiology
- **Gait Dynamics**:
  - Upright, poised posture with crown reaching tall.
  - Continuous double-support phase (at least one foot always in contact).
  - Smooth heel-to-midfoot-to-toe roll; gentle natural reciprocal arm swing.

### Silhouette Translation
- Measured, graceful walking silhouette evoking meditative presence and grounded ease.
